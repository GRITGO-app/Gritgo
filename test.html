    Welcome to GRITGO!

    Here is your complete guide on how to use the app:

    1. Daily Habits

    • Add/Edit: Click "Add Habit" to create a new habit. Click "Edit habits" to rename, reorder (↑↓), or delete habits.
    • Checkbox: Tap the number box next to a habit to complete it. It turns blue with a checkmark.
    • Search: Use the search bar to find a habit by its name or number.

    2. Heatmap & Past Dates

    • Heatmap: Each box represents a day. If you complete all habits, the box glows in Aqua color.
    • Past Edit: Forgot to check a habit yesterday? Tap on any past box in the heatmap to open that day and edit it.

    3. Quick Actions (Below Heatmap)

    • Check All (Blue check): Instantly checks all habits for today in one click. Great for a perfect day!
    • Reset All (Black reset): Unchecks all habits for today if you made a mistake.

    4. Stats & Progress

    • Overall %: Shows the percentage of habits you completed TODAY.
    • Challenge Progress: Shows how many DAYS have passed in your challenge.
    • Streak & Perfect Days: Tracks how many consecutive days you completed 100% of your habits.

    5. Extras & Insights

    • Achievements: Unlock badges by maintaining streaks.
    • Calendar & Notes: View your monthly progress and write a daily note.

    6. Sleep & Backup

    • Sleep Tracker: Log your sleep to see your Brain State. (Note: It remains Locked until your challenge starts).
    • Backup: Always export your data (JSON) so you don't lose progress if you clear your browser.
