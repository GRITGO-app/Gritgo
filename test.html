    Welcome to GRITGO!

    Here is your complete guide on how to use the app:

    1. Daily Habits & Search

    • Add/Edit: Click "Add Habit" to create. Click "Edit habits" to rename or delete.
    • Checkbox: Tap the number box next to a habit to complete it.
    • Search: Use the search bar to find a habit quickly.

    2. Advanced Timer

    • Custom Time: Choose from 5m, 30m, 1h, or type your own time.
    • Auto-Check: When the timer ends, a sound plays and the habit is automatically checked!

    AI

    3. Smart AI Coach

    • Voice Input: Click the Mic icon and speak in any language.
    • 📷 Send Photos: Click the Image icon to send a photo to AI.
    • AI Reviews: Get your Weekly & Monthly progress analyzed by the AI.

    4. Heatmap & Edits

    • Heatmap: Each box represents a day. Complete all habits to make it glow Aqua.
    • Past Edit: Tap on any past box in the heatmap to open that day and edit it.

    5. Progress & Charts

    • Extras & Insights: Opens the dashboard where you can see your Weekly Line Chart and 7-Day Habits bar.
    • Day Streak: Tracks how many consecutive days you completed 100% of your habits.
    • Quick Actions: Use the Blue check to mark all done, and Black reset to uncheck.

    6. Sleep & Backup

    • Sleep Tracker: Log your sleep to see your Brain State. (Locked until challenge starts).
    • Backup: Always export your data from Settings so you don't lose progress.
